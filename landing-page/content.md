## Core capabilities

Section eyebrow: **WHAT DRAFTLY DOES**

Headline:

**The repetitive work moves faster.**  
**The legal reasoning stays reviewable.**

### Multilingual document processing

Process scanned and digital documents containing Sinhala, Tamil and English. Draftly detects document boundaries, corrects page orientation, reads the text and preserves the original page as the source of truth.

Interface action: **Open original page**

### Document classification

Separate a mixed upload into its legal documents and identify deeds, survey plans, assessment notices, registry records, title reports and other supporting material before extraction begins.

Interface action: **Review document type**

### Source-linked fact extraction

Extract names, dates, property descriptions, deed numbers, notary details, consideration values and other matter facts. Each proposed value includes its source document, page and evidence for lawyer verification.

Interface action: **Verify fact**

### Cross-document checks

Compare facts across the matter and surface conflicts that deserve attention: name variations, inconsistent extents, missing deed links, date conflicts, plan mismatches and incomplete supporting records.

Interface action: **Inspect conflict**

### Chain-of-title workspace

Organise transfers and related instruments into a reviewable title history. Lawyers can confirm, correct or reject each link while keeping the underlying deeds close at hand.

Interface action: **Review title chain**

### Statutory and case-law research

Retrieve relevant provisions and authorities for the legal issue being reviewed. Results retain section-level citations and source text so the lawyer can inspect what the system relied on.

Interface action: **View authorities**

### Draft preparation

Prepare first drafts of title reports, schedules, clauses, forms, missing-document checklists and evidence packs from facts the lawyer has reviewed. Generated content remains editable and does not become final until approved.

Interface action: **Prepare draft**

### Matter history and audit trail

Record uploads, extracted values, corrections, approvals and document versions. See what changed, who reviewed it and which evidence supported the final output.

Interface action: **View matter history**

Headline:

**Useful enough to assist.**  
**Transparent enough to question.**

Intro:

Draftly is designed as a legal workbench, not an autonomous lawyer. The interface distinguishes source material, machine-proposed information and lawyer-approved facts throughout the matter.

### 01 â€” The source remains visible

Material facts link back to the original document and page. A reviewer can inspect the evidence instead of relying on an unexplained answer.

### 02 â€” Proposals are not approvals

Extracted facts, detected conflicts and generated text remain unverified until a lawyer reviews them. The system does not silently treat a model output as a legal conclusion.

### 03 â€” Uncertainty is surfaced

Low-confidence text, conflicting evidence and missing information are shown as review items. Draftly can abstain or request further review when the available evidence is insufficient.

### 04 â€” Legal sources are cited

Research outputs retain references to the relevant enactment, section and source text so the legal basis can be checked independently.

### 05 â€” Changes are recorded

Corrections, approvals and generated versions form part of the matter history, helping teams understand how the final output was produced.

### 06 â€” Professional judgment stays central

Draftly supports legal work. It does not provide unsupervised legal advice, replace independent verification or shift professional responsibility away from the lawyer.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Draftly replace a lawyer?

No. Draftly organises documents, proposes structured facts, runs checks and prepares editable drafts. A qualified legal practitioner must verify the evidence, apply professional judgment and approve the final work.

### Is Draftly a general legal chatbot?

No. It is a workflow and research platform built around source documents, structured checks and cited legal material. It is currently focused on Sri Lankan conveyancing.

### Which languages does it support?

The document pipeline is being developed for Sinhala, Tamil and English legal documents. Performance can vary with scan quality, handwriting, layout and document type, so extracted content remains subject to review.

### Can I see where an extracted fact came from?

Yes. Draftly is designed to link a proposed fact to its source document and page so the reviewer can inspect and correct it.

### Does it generate legal documents automatically?

Draftly can prepare editable first drafts from reviewed matter data. Generated documents must still be checked and finalised by the responsible lawyer.

### Is the statutory coverage complete?

Not yet. The current research corpus focuses on legislation and authorities relevant to the initial conveyancing scope. Coverage is expanded and validated incrementally.

### Can researchers collaborate with the project?

Yes. We welcome conversations about statutory retrieval, multilingual legal document processing, legal datasets and expert-in-the-loop evaluation.
