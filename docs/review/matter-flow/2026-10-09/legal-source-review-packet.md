## Proposed use to review

- Internal research: index exact reviewed official source text, retrieve policy-permitted supporting snippets, and link to the official original through a versioned controlled release.
- Public catalogue: independently approved metadata and official source links only. No full-text publication or original-file download permission is requested here.
- Preserve each source's language, publication/certification dates, exact amendment relations and review locators. Unknown commencement remains unknown. Partial amendments are not whole-source replacement or automatically consolidated law.
- Verify page derivatives against the exact original and record warnings. The 50 scanned pages remain blocked from new text indexing until OCR and derivative review are complete.
- Record the responsible source/legal owner's identity, approval reference and exact audiences/policies. Production signing-key custody remains with the maintainer; no key is generated or configured by the agent.

The source-governance service plan requires independent rights/content review and
audience approval before release. Architecture approval and the client's data
processing letter do not fill these separate source-review records. Every acquired
record currently has pending rights/content review, blocked indexing and no
publication approval. Implementation and synthetic verification can continue with
